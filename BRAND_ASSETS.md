# Brand assets

The dashboard shows each platform's official mark to indicate where a tracked post comes from
(`apps/dashboard/src/components/platform.tsx`). The marks are used unmodified (no recolouring,
distortion or effects) and are trademarks of their owners. Post Tracker is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Reddit, X or LinkedIn.

| X logo, black/white                        | Path data from Simple Icons v15 (`x`), sourced from https://x.com                                                          | Icon data CC0-1.0; trademark of X Corp. — follow https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit (black or white only) |
| LinkedIn "In" bug, LinkedIn Blue `#0A66C2` | Vector published on LinkedIn's brand site https://brand.linkedin.com (the official `in-logo.zip` there contains only PNGs) | Trademark of LinkedIn Corporation — follow https://brand.linkedin.com/policies                                         |

Simple Icons no longer distributes the LinkedIn mark (removed at LinkedIn's request), which is
why it comes from LinkedIn directly. If these marks are ever used outside a local tool (e.g. a
public product or store listing), review each company's current guidelines first.
